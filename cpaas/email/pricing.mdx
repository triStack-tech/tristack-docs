**1.5 paise per recipient.** No monthly fee, no minimum, no charge for verifying a domain or
for turning the product on.

<Warning>
  **From 1 December 2026: 2.5 paise per recipient, plus 2 paise per started MB of attachments
  above 100 KB, per recipient.** A single-recipient email then settles at 3 paise. See
  [Attachments, from 1 December 2026](#attachments-from-1-december-2026) and the
  [price changes](/changelog).
</Warning>

<Note>
  A wallet holds whole paise and cannot be debited a fraction of one, so the total for a
  message is rounded **up** to the next paise. A single-recipient email therefore settles at
  **2 paise**, and larger sends average out to the true rate: ten recipients cost 15 paise,
  a hundred cost 150. From 1 December 2026: 3 paise, 25 paise and 250 paise.
</Note>

## Per recipient, not per message

This is the part that surprises people, so it is worth being blunt about it. One email to
fifty people is fifty deliveries and costs fifty times one email to one person.

`To`, `Cc` and `Bcc` all count the same. An address that appears in more than one of them is
one delivery and is counted once.

| What you send | Recipients | Cost | From 1 December 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One address in `to` | 1 | 2 paise (1.5, rounded up) | 3 paise (2.5, rounded up) |
| Ten in `to` | 10 | 15 paise | 25 paise |
| Five in `to`, three in `cc` | 8 | 12 paise | 20 paise |
| One address in both `to` and `cc` | 1 | 2 paise | 3 paise |

Every response tells you what it charged and over how many recipients, so you can always check
the arithmetic:

```json
{ "id": "rle_9f2c…", "messageId": "0109…", "costPaise": 12, "recipients": 8 }
```

## When you are charged

At the moment the message is accepted by the provider. The amount is held before sending and
settled after, so:

- **A message that is accepted is charged.** Delivery happens afterwards and a bounce is not a
  refund: the send was performed and paid for. That is how every email provider works.
- **A message the provider refuses costs nothing.** The hold is released whole and your balance
  is untouched.
- **A message rejected before sending costs nothing.** A bad address, an unverified domain, or
  a rate limit never reaches the provider.

If your balance cannot cover the send it is refused with `insufficient_balance` and the
required amount, before anything is sent.

## Batches

A [batch](/cpaas/email/templates) is billed the same way: per recipient, summed across every
message in it, at the same rate. A batch is a convenience, not a discount.

The rounding is applied once to the batch total rather than once per message, so a batch is
never dearer than the same messages sent one at a time:

| What you send | Recipients | Cost | From 1 December 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Three single-recipient messages, separately | 3 | 6 paise (2 + 2 + 2) | 9 paise (3 + 3 + 3) |
| The same three as one batch | 3 | 5 paise (4.5, rounded up) | 8 paise (7.5, rounded up) |

Only the messages that were accepted are billed. A message that failed inside a batch costs
nothing, and the balance for the whole batch is held before any of it is sent: if it cannot be
covered, nothing goes out.

## Attachments, from 1 December 2026

Until 30 November 2026 attachments are not billed. From 1 December 2026, a message whose
attachments come to more than 100 KB adds **2 paise per started MB above the first 100 KB, for
each recipient**, and the send's total is rounded up once, as before:

| What you send | From 1 December 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| One recipient, a 60 KB attachment | 3 paise (2.5, rounded up) |
| One recipient, 1.5 MB of attachments | 7 paise: 2.5 + 2 x 2 started MB, 6.5 rounded up |
| Four recipients, 1.5 MB of attachments | 26 paise: 4 x (2.5 + 2 x 2) |

The [send response](/api-reference/cpaas/email/send) says what each send cost, so the arithmetic
can always be checked.

## Rate limits

Two limits apply, and exceeding either returns `429` with a `Retry-After` header:

- **5 sends per second per project.**
- An additional platform ceiling.

A batch counts as its **message count**, not as one request, so 500 messages spend 500 of that
allowance: at 5 per second, the next send is refused for 100 seconds. `Retry-After` says how
long.

These protect deliverability for your domain and everyone you send to.

## Checking what you spent

Email appears in **Usage** alongside Manifold AI and the other CPaaS channels, with recipients
as the unit. Each row records the rate it was charged at, so a historical charge stays
checkable even after prices change.
