title: "Voice pricing"
description: "Answered calls cost Rs 0.40 per started 30 seconds, new speech 3 paise per character once, and a project pays Rs 200 the first time it switches Voice on. Unanswered calls cost nothing."
---

Voice, the Tristack CPaaS calling channel, meters two things: **speech** and **answered
calls**. Making a recording costs 3 paise per character, once. Every answered call costs
40 paise per started 30 seconds of the call. A call nobody answers costs nothing.

| Charge | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | **20000 paise (Rs 200)** | Once, the first time Voice is switched on for a project |
| New speech | **3 paise per character** | Once, when a recording is prepared. Never again for that recording |
| Answered call | **40 paise per started 30 seconds** | Every answered call, measured from answer to hang-up as the carrier reports it |
| Call not answered | **Nothing** | Busy, rung out, failed or cancelled: a call nobody answered is not a charge |

There is no monthly fee, no charge for a call nobody answers, no charge for reading a job and
no charge for listing voices.

<Note>
  Renting a phone number of your own is separate, optional, and the only recurring charge on
  the platform: Rs 200 plus Rs 36 GST per number per month. See
  [phone numbers](/cpaas/voice/numbers). Until a project rents a number of its own, its calls
  go out from a shared Tristack number, which has no rental charge. Its calls are charged like
  any other.
</Note>

## 3 paise per character of new speech

Speech is recorded, not streamed, and a recording is paid for **once**: at 3 paise per
character of the text, counted after leading and trailing whitespace is trimmed.

The 65-character message

> Clinic closed tomorrow for Diwali. We reopen on Thursday at 9 am.

costs `65 x 3 = 195 paise` to record, whether it then goes to one phone or five hundred.

**You do not pay it twice for the same words.** Sending an `audioId`, or re-sending wording
your account has already recorded in the same voice, skips synthesis entirely and the
recording charge with it. The calls are charged either way, so the recording is the part of
a Voice bill your code decides: see [reusing a recording](/cpaas/voice/reusing-audio).

<Warning>
  A recording is charged when it is **prepared**, not when it is heard. Record a new message,
  send it to a hundred numbers and have nobody pick up, and you still pay the 195 paise. The
  unanswered calls cost nothing. The recording was still made.
</Warning>

## 40 paise per started 30 seconds of an answered call

A call is charged only if it was answered, on its length from answer to hang-up as the
carrier reports it, in steps of 30 seconds. A step that has started counts in full.

| Answered call lasts | Steps | Paise |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 to 30 seconds | 1 | 40 |
| 31 to 60 seconds | 2 | 80 |
| 61 to 90 seconds | 3 | 120 |
| Every further 30 seconds, or part of it | 1 more | 40 more |

The 65-character message above plays in about 6 seconds, so an answered call that carries it
is one step: 40 paise.

| Call status | Meaning | Charged |
|---|---|---|
| `Completed` | Answered | 40 paise per started 30 seconds of `durationSeconds` |
| `Busy` | Engaged | Nothing |
| `NoAnswer` | Rang out | Nothing |
| `Failed` | The network or the provider refused it | Nothing |
| `Cancelled` | Never placed | Nothing |

Every call carries its own `costPaise` in the job body: its started 30-second steps times 40
when it was answered, and `0` otherwise. `GET /v1/cpaas/voice/voices` reports the rate as
`pricePerCallPaise` (`40`) and the step as `callStepSeconds` (`30`). Read them rather than
hardcoding either.

**Every call has a time limit: the recording's length plus 30 seconds.** A call cannot run
longer than that, which caps what any one call can cost. A 6-second recording can never make
a call longer than 36 seconds, or cost more than two steps (80 paise).

The dashboard's **test call** rings a real phone and replays a recording you already have, so
it carries no recording charge. The call itself is charged like any answered call.

## Activation: Rs 200, once, per project

Switching **Voice** on for a project charges 20000 paise the first time it happens for
that project. It buys the project its place on the outbound line and its registration with
the carriers, both of which we are billed for monthly whether or not you place a single call.

<Warning>
  It is not a phone number. A number of your own is
  [rented separately](/cpaas/voice/numbers), monthly, and is optional.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Switching it off and on again is free" icon="toggle-on">
    The fee is stamped on the project the first time it is paid and the stamp is never
    cleared. Flipping the toggle later, however many times, costs nothing.
    `relayActivationPaid` on the project tells you which projects have already paid.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A second project is a second Rs 200" icon="folder-plus">
    Each project is registered with the carriers on its own, so one project's standing is
    never another project's problem. If you do not need that separation, do not create a
    separate project: one project can serve several campaigns, and recordings are shared
    account-wide anyway.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It is charged before the switch flips" icon="wallet">
    If the wallet cannot cover the fee, the answer is `402 insufficient_balance` with
    `requiredPaise: 20000` and the project is left exactly as it was, rather than switched on
    for free. Top up and flip it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where it appears" icon="receipt">
    In the wallet ledger as a `Debit`, referenced `relay-activation-` followed by the
    project id **with its hyphens removed**: project `8f14e45f-ceea-467a-9a1f-6b3c1a2d4e57`
    gives `relay-activation-8f14e45fceea467a9a1f6b3c1a2d4e57`. Strip the hyphens before
    matching, or the lookup silently finds nothing. It is deliberately **not** in
    `GET /api/v1/usage`: that endpoint reports product consumption, and the activation fee is
    account spend rather than something you consumed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How the money moves

<Steps>
  <Step title="A hold goes on before anything is dialed">
    The recording charge, `newCharacters x 3`, if this request is the one making a new
    recording, plus, for every recipient, the call charge at the recording's length: as if
    everybody answered and each call lasted as long as the recording. An existing recording or
    an upload uses its known length; new text uses an estimate from the length of the text.
    The hold is reserved the moment the request is accepted, and `estimatedCostPaise` on the
    response is that amount.

    If the balance cannot cover it, the send is refused with `402 insufficient_balance` and
    `requiredPaise`. That check happens before any provider is called, so a refused send
    costs nothing at all.
  </Step>

  <Step title="A replay holds for its calls">
    Replaying a recording has no recording charge, but its calls still cost money, so it is
    held and settled like any other send and appears on the ledger like any other job. It
    needs a balance that covers every recipient's call: on an empty wallet it is refused with
    `402 insufficient_balance`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="It settles at what the answered calls took">
    When the last call ends, the job is charged the recording charge, if it made the
    recording, plus 40 paise per started 30 seconds of each answered call. The rest of the
    hold is returned, so every recipient who did not answer hands their share back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Worked examples

Every message in these examples is 71 characters or shorter and plays in about 6 seconds, so
each answered call is one 30-second step.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="First message on a new project">
    A new project, three test numbers, a 65-character announcement. Two people answer.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Activation, once for this project | | 20,000 |
    | Recording, once for this wording | 65 x 3 | 195 |
    | Two answered calls, one step each | 2 x 40 | 80 |
    | One unanswered call | 1 x 0 | 0 |
    | **Wallet spent** | | **20,275** |

    **Rs 202.75**, of which Rs 200 never happens again for this project. The job's own
    `costPaise` is 275: the activation fee is a separate wallet debit, not part of the job.
    Had nobody answered, the job would have cost 195.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Broadcast to 500">
    One 65-character message, 500 numbers, 340 answer. The project is already activated.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Held at send | 195 + 500 x 40 | 20,195 |
    | Recording | 65 x 3 | 195 |
    | 340 answered calls | 340 x 40 | 13,600 |
    | 160 unanswered calls | 160 x 0 | 0 |
    | **Charged** | | **13,795** |
    | Returned at settlement | 20,195 - 13,795 | 6,400 |

    **Rs 137.95** to reach 340 people. The 160 who did not answer cost nothing, and their
    Rs 64 of the hold went back to the wallet.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="The same broadcast again">
    Next month, same wording, sent by `audioId` (or by sending the identical text again) to
    the same 500 numbers. 340 answer again.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Held at send | 500 x 40 | 20,000 |
    | Recording | already yours | 0 |
    | 340 answered calls | 340 x 40 | 13,600 |
    | 160 unanswered calls | 160 x 0 | 0 |
    | **Charged** | | **13,600** |

    **Rs 136.00**, all of it calls. Reusing the recording saved the Rs 1.95 of speech; the
    calls cost what they cost the first time. The job is held, settled and on the ledger like
    any other, so the wallet has to cover the hold before the first phone rings.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="One personalized reminder">
    A single 71-character message naming one customer, who answers.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Recording, unique to this person | 71 x 3 | 213 |
    | One answered call | 1 x 40 | 40 |
    | **Charged** | | **253** |

    **Rs 2.53**, or Rs 2.13 if nobody answers. Personalized sending is where a large Voice
    bill comes from: every message is different, so every message is a new recording. Five
    hundred of these, all answered, cost Rs 1,265 (Rs 1,065 of speech and Rs 200 of calls);
    one 71-character message broadcast to the same five hundred, all answered, costs
    Rs 202.13. The calls are the same either way. The recordings are the difference.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What costs nothing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Every call nobody answers" icon="phone">
    Busy, rung out, failed or never placed. Only an answered call is charged, however many
    recipients a job has.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Any request that is refused" icon="ban">
    A validation failure (`400`), a product that is switched off (`403`), a wallet that
    cannot cover the hold (`402`), or a speech provider that fails or refuses your text
    (`502`). The balance check runs before any provider is called, and every `502` releases
    the hold in full. A refused send never costs a paisa.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recording, on a replay" icon="rotate-left">
    Preparing the audio is free on every send after the first, whether you replay by
    `audioId` or send the identical text and voice again, and an uploaded MP3 never carries a
    recording charge. The answered calls on such a send are charged as usual: a replay saves
    the recording, not the calls.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Duplicate numbers in one request" icon="copy">
    The same person written twice in one `to` array is normalized, deduplicated and dialed
    once. `recipients` on the response tells you how many the request really had.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reading jobs, listing voices, playing samples" icon="magnifying-glass">
    `GET /v1/cpaas/voice/{jobId}` and `GET /v1/cpaas/voice/voices` are free and unmetered,
    poll them as often as you like. The dashboard's in-browser voice samples are free too.
    Its test call is not: the call is charged like any answered call.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The calls of a job we could not finish" icon="rotate">
    If the service restarts mid-job, the job ends as `Failed` with `error: "interrupted"` and
    nothing is redialed. You are charged for the recording, if this job made it, and for the
    calls that were answered before it stopped, and nothing else.

    The same goes for a job stopped because the account was disabled or the project archived
    after it was accepted: it ends as `Failed` with `account_disabled` or `project_archived`,
    the calls not yet placed are `Cancelled` and cost nothing, and only the recording charge
    and the calls already answered stand.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Where to see what you spent

| Surface | Shows |
|---|---|
| The job body, `costPaise` | The final charge for that job: the recording charge if this job made it, plus every call's own `costPaise`. Each call's `costPaise` is 40 per started 30 seconds if it was answered, and `0` otherwise. |
| `GET /api/v1/wallet/transactions` | The ledger. A Voice job appears as a `Hold` then a `Debit`, referenced by its `rvj_…` id. A job that replayed a recording appears too, because its calls cost money. The activation fee is a `Debit` referenced `relay-activation-<projectId>`; a number rental is referenced `relay-number-<numberId>`. |
| `GET /api/v1/usage?product=relay_voice` | Per day, project and voice. `units` is calls placed, which is a volume rather than a bill: `costPaise` is the money, the recordings plus the answered calls. |

<Tip>
  Estimating a campaign before you run it: `characters x 3`, once per **distinct** message,
  plus `40 x started 30-second steps` for each call you expect to be answered, plus `20000`
  if the project has never had Voice switched on, plus `23600` a month for each number you
  rent. Calls nobody answers add nothing. The wallet still has to cover the hold, which counts
  every recipient as answering.
</Tip>
