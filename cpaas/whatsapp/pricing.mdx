## Two bills, not one

This is the only CPaaS channel where somebody else also charges you, so it is worth
separating clearly.

| Who charges | For what | Where it lands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Meta** | WhatsApp messages, billed separately | Your own Meta invoice, at their rates |
| **Tristack** | Sending and verifying the code | Your Tristack wallet |

Meta's rate is theirs to set and theirs to bill, and it varies by country. We never see it and
never add to it.

## What Tristack charges

**20 paise per code sent.** A flat fee, whatever the destination country.

Charged on the **send**, never on the verify. The send is what Meta bills you for and what we
did work for; checking a code is free and unlimited within the attempt cap, so a
person fumbling their code four times costs you nothing extra.

The balance is held before anything reaches WhatsApp. If it cannot be covered you get
`insufficient_balance` with the amount required, and nothing is sent.

## What is not charged

- **A resend inside the resend window.** It returns the live code's `id` with
  `costPaise: 0`, because nothing was sent, by us or by Meta.
- **A send WhatsApp refused.** An unapproved template, an unreachable number, a token that
  no longer works: the hold is released whole and you pay nothing. This applies to every
  `502` from this endpoint.
- **Verification, always.** However many attempts, up to the cap.
- **A send refused by a rate limit.** Nothing is held, so nothing has to come back.

## Reading it back

Each send carries `costPaise` in its own response, and the wallet ledger records the charge
against the same `otp_…` id, so a line on an invoice can be traced to the exact request that
made it. WhatsApp appears in **Usage** alongside Manifold AI and the other channels.

<Note>
  The rate is recorded on every send as it stood at that moment, so a price that changes later
  never rewrites what an earlier send cost.
</Note>
