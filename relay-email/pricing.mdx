description: "Five paise per recipient, charged when the message is accepted. What counts as a recipient, what a failure costs, and how to check a charge."
---

**5 paise per recipient.** No monthly fee, no minimum, no charge for verifying a domain or for
turning the product on.

## Per recipient, not per message

This is the part that surprises people, so it is worth being blunt about it. One email to
fifty people is fifty deliveries and costs fifty times one email to one person.

`To`, `Cc` and `Bcc` all count the same. An address that appears in more than one of them is
one delivery and is counted once.

| What you send | Recipients | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One address in `to` | 1 | 5 paise |
| Ten in `to` | 10 | 50 paise |
| Five in `to`, three in `cc` | 8 | 40 paise |
| One address in both `to` and `cc` | 1 | 5 paise |

Every response tells you what it charged and over how many recipients, so you can always check
the arithmetic:

```json
{ "id": "rle_9f2c…", "messageId": "0109…", "costPaise": 40, "recipients": 8 }
```

## When you are charged

At the moment the message is accepted by the provider. The amount is held before sending and
settled after, so:

- **A message that is accepted is charged.** Delivery happens afterwards and a bounce is not a
  refund: the send was performed and paid for. That is how every email provider works.
- **A message the provider refuses costs nothing.** The hold is released whole and your balance
  is untouched.
- **A message rejected before sending costs nothing.** A bad address, an unverified domain, or
  a rate limit never reaches the provider.

If your balance cannot cover the send it is refused with `insufficient_balance` and the
required amount, before anything is sent.

## Rate limits

Two limits apply, and exceeding either returns `429` with a `Retry-After` header:

- **5 sends per second per project.**
- A platform-wide ceiling shared across all customers.

These exist because email sending capacity is a shared, rationed resource, and one caller
saturating it would degrade delivery for everyone including Tristack's own account emails.

## Checking what you spent

Relay Email appears in **Usage** alongside Manifold and Relay Voice, with recipients as the
unit. Each row records the rate it was charged at, so a historical charge stays checkable even
after prices change.
