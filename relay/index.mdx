Relay is how a Tristack project reaches a person. Every channel takes the same API key, settles
against the same wallet, and is switched on per project, but each one is its own product with
its own endpoint, its own request shape and its own price.

## What they share

**One key.** A project API key works across every Relay channel it has switched on, and across
Manifold too. There is no per-product credential.

**One wallet.** A call, an email and a Manifold request all draw on the same balance. There is
no per-product or per-project balance to top up separately.

**One switch each.** Every channel is enabled per project, independently. Turning Relay Email
on does nothing to Relay Voice, and a key on a project with the channel off is refused with
that channel's own `..._disabled` code.

## What they do not share

**Nothing passes between them automatically.** Relay Voice will not read an email out loud, and
Relay Email will not send a transcript of a call. Text produced by one product is just text to
another, and passing it across is something your code does deliberately.

**Prices are per channel and not comparable.** A voice call is priced by the characters it speaks, an
email per recipient, a WhatsApp code per code sent. There is no shared unit, and a rupee buys a
different amount of each.

**Relay WhatsApp has a second bill that the others do not.** You hold the Meta relationship, so
Meta charges you for each conversation directly, on top of what Tristack charges. Relay Voice
and Relay Email have one bill: ours.

**Request shapes differ.** They all use `camelCase` on the wire, unlike Manifold's
`snake_case`, but the fields themselves have nothing in common: Relay Voice takes phone numbers
and a voice alias, Relay Email takes addresses and a verified sending domain, and Relay
WhatsApp takes one number and returns an id to verify against.

## Relay and Manifold

Manifold is the other product, and it is not part of Relay. Manifold generates text; Relay
delivers messages to people. They share an account, a wallet and a key scheme and nothing else.
[The products page](/platform/products) draws that line in detail.
