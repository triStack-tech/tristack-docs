<Info>

<Update label="2026-08-31" description="Relay Voice calls are free, and you can rent your own number">

  **Calls are no longer metered.** `pricePerCallPaise` is `0`. Every call, in every outcome,
  now costs nothing, and speech is the only per-use charge Relay Voice has: 3 paise per
  character, once, when a recording is prepared.

  What this changes for you:

  - A send that replays an existing recording is **completely free**. It takes no hold, makes
    no debit and leaves no row in the ledger, so such a send works on an empty wallet. If you
    reconcile Relay Voice jobs against wallet transactions, expect jobs with no matching rows.
  - `estimatedCostPaise` now equals the final `costPaise` rather than being a ceiling, because
    the only charge is known before the first phone rings. Nothing comes back at settlement.
  - `costPaise` on an individual call is `0` in every state, including `Completed`. Summing
    that column no longer tells you anything about your bill; count statuses to measure reach.
  - Broadcasting got dramatically cheaper and personalizing did not. One 65-character message
    to 500 people is Rs 1.95, where it was Rs 201.95. Five hundred personalized messages are
    unchanged at Rs 1,065. See [reusing a recording](/relay-voice/reusing-audio).
  - The one-time Rs 200 activation per project is unchanged, but it never was a phone number
    and the docs no longer say it was.

  `pricePerCallPaise` stays on `GET /v1/relay/voice/voices` and on usage rows. Read it rather
  than hardcoding the zero.

  **Rent your own number**

  - `GET /v1/relay/voice/numbers/available`, `POST /v1/relay/voice/numbers` and
    `PATCH /v1/relay/voice/numbers/{id}` search for, rent and stop renewing an Indian number,
    so calls go out from your number instead of a shared one. Keys need the new
    `relay-voice:numbers` scope.
  - **Rs 236 per number per month**, as Rs 200 plus Rs 36 GST, shown split on the ledger. This
    is the only recurring charge on the platform.
  - Indian numbers are sold only to registered businesses. Verify once per account through
    `POST /v1/relay/voice/compliance` with an incorporation and a GST certificate; a person
    reviews it over a few days.
  - **There is no grace period.** The renewal is decided the day before it falls due, and a
    wallet that cannot cover it, or auto-renew left off, releases the number permanently. The
    account owner is warned by email seven, three and two days before, the last landing a day
    ahead of the decision.
  - New error codes: `compliance_required`, `compliance_pending`, `compliance_exists`,
    `number_unavailable`, `document_too_large`, `unsupported_document_type`. See
    [phone numbers](/relay-voice/numbers).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-22" description="Embedded top-up window and automatic top-ups">

  Two new ways to put money in the wallet. Both credit the same account balance every
  product spends from, and neither changes how a request is priced, held or settled.

  **Top up from your own app**

  - `POST /v1/wallet/topup-sessions` mints a single-use permit from your backend, and your
    page opens a Tristack-hosted top-up window with it, so the account holder adds money
    without ever coming to the dashboard. The permit expires in fifteen minutes and rides in
    the URL fragment, so it never reaches a server log.
  - `GET /v1/wallet` reads the balance with the same project API key: `balancePaise`,
    `minTopUpPaise`, `lowBalance`, and where automatic top-up stands. Amounts are integer
    paise as everywhere else, so Rs 100 is `10000`.
  - Both need switching on per project in the dashboard, with at least one allowed origin.
    See [Top up from your own app](/platform/embedded-topup).

  **Automatic top-ups**

  - Set a threshold on the Wallet page in the dashboard and the wallet refills itself from a
    saved payment method. Dashboard only, never the API-key surface.
  - A charge is never immediate. RBI's e-mandate framework requires notice before every
    recurring debit, so a tripped threshold sends an email and the charge follows roughly
    24 hours later. Size the threshold to cover about a day of your own spend, or the refill
    lands after the outage instead of before it.
  - Manual top-ups are unaffected whether automatic top-ups are unavailable, off or
    suspended. See [Automatic top-ups](/platform/auto-topup).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-21" description="Manifold prices corrected to the published rate">

  <Warning>
    **Manifold prices went up by about 11 percent.** Relay Voice is unaffected, and no past
    usage was re-billed.
  </Warning>

  The deployment was running an older set of rates than the published ones, so calls were
  being billed about 10 percent under the published price. It now bills the published
  rates, which is what `GET /v1/manifold/models` has always been the authority for.

  The tables on these pages were wrong in the other direction, and listed prices about
  10 percent under what was actually charged. They have been regenerated and now match the
  endpoint.

  What this means for you:

  - `nova-micro` input moves from 0.35 to **0.38** paise per 1000 tokens, output from 1.39
    to **1.54**. Other models move by the same proportion.
  - Read `GET /v1/manifold/models` if you display or budget against prices. It reports the
    per-1000-token paise for every model your key can call, and it is the authority. These
    tables are a snapshot.
  - Nothing about the request or response shape changed.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-21" description="Relay Voice documented, pages reorganised">

  Relay Voice is documented here for the first time, and these pages were rebuilt around
  two products instead of one. Nothing in the Manifold API changed with this entry: what
  moved is the documentation.

  **Relay Voice**

  - `POST /v1/relay/voice` takes Indian mobile numbers and either `text` to speak or an
    `audioId` to replay, and answers `202` with a `jobId`. The calls are dialled in the
    background.
  - `GET /v1/relay/voice/{jobId}` reports the job and every call under it.
    `Completed` means every call reached a terminal state, not that anyone answered.
  - `GET /v1/relay/voice/voices` lists the voice aliases you may name in `voice`.
  - Pricing: ₹200 once per project the first time Relay Voice is switched on, then
    40 paise per answered call and 3 paise per character of newly synthesized speech.
    A phone that rings out costs nothing. See [Relay Voice pricing](/relay-voice/pricing).

  **Where the Manifold pages went**

  | Was | Now |

<Update label="2026-08-17" description="First public release of the documentation">

  First public release of the documentation, describing Tristack Manifold as it runs today.

  **API surface**

  - `POST /v1/manifold/messages`: text and image messages, optional `system` prompt,
    `max_tokens` (default 4096, cap 65536), `temperature`, `top_p`, `stop_sequences`, and
    `stream` for server-sent events.
  - `GET /v1/manifold/models`: the catalog with per-model rates in paise per 1000 tokens.
  - API-key authentication on both, as `Authorization: Bearer tsk_...` or `X-Api-Key`.

  **Catalog**

  - 54 aliases across 19 families, converted at 90.0 INR per USD.
  - Five aliases are switched off on this deployment: `opus-5`, `opus-4-8`, `sonnet-5`,
    `sonnet-4-6` and `haiku-4-5`. Account-level access is still being finalised, so
    `GET /v1/manifold/models` does not list them and a call answers `400 unknown_model`,
    the same answer a misspelt alias gets. The other 49 serve normally.
  - 21 aliases accept image blocks. The list is on the [models page](/manifold/models).

  **Billing**

  - Prepaid wallet per account, in integer paise, minimum top-up 10000 paise (₹100).
  - Hold before the call, settle on real token usage afterwards, difference returned.
  - Failed calls cost nothing. A client abort mid-stream settles, for the whole input
    estimate plus whatever output had been delivered.

  **Documentation**

  - Getting started, authentication, streaming, vision, the full API reference, the error
    catalog, the model catalog, the billing model and how to get support.
  - Model and price tables are generated from a captured `GET /v1/manifold/models`
    response, so they match the deployment they were captured from.

</Update>
