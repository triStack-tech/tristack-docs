title: "Relay Voice pricing"
description: "You pay to turn text into speech, and not to make the calls: 3 paise per character, once, plus Rs 200 the first time a project switches Relay Voice on."
---

Relay Voice meters one thing: **speech**. Making a recording costs 3 paise per character,
once. Dialing it costs nothing, however many phones it reaches and whoever picks up.

| Charge | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | **20000 paise (Rs 200)** | Once, the first time Relay Voice is switched on for a project |
| New speech | **3 paise per character** | Once, when a recording is prepared. Never again for that recording |
| Calls | **Nothing** | Answered, busy, rung out or refused, a call is not a charge |

There is no monthly fee, no per-recipient fee, no charge for reading a job and no charge for
listing voices.

<Note>
  Renting a phone number of your own is separate, optional, and the only recurring charge on
  the platform: Rs 200 plus Rs 36 GST per number per month. See
  [phone numbers](/relay-voice/numbers). Without one your calls go out from a shared Tristack
  number, which costs nothing beyond the activation fee.
</Note>

## 3 paise per character of new speech

Speech is recorded, not streamed, and a recording is paid for **once**: at 3 paise per
character of the text, counted after leading and trailing whitespace is trimmed.

The 65-character message

> Clinic closed tomorrow for Diwali. We reopen on Thursday at 9 am.

costs `65 x 3 = 195 paise` to record, whether it then goes to one phone or five hundred.

**You do not pay it twice for the same words.** Sending an `audioId`, or re-sending wording
your account has already recorded in the same voice, skips synthesis entirely and the charge
with it. That makes it the only variable in a Relay Voice bill: see
[reusing a recording](/relay-voice/reusing-audio).

<Warning>
  A recording is charged when it is **prepared**, not when it is heard. Record a new message,
  send it to a hundred numbers and have nobody pick up, and you still pay the 195 paise. The
  calls were free. The recording was not.
</Warning>

## The calls are free

Every call status costs the same: nothing.

| Call status | Meaning | Charged |
|---|---|---|
| `Completed` | Answered | Nothing |
| `Busy` | Engaged | Nothing |
| `NoAnswer` | Rang out | Nothing |
| `Failed` | The network or the provider refused it | Nothing |
| `Cancelled` | Never placed | Nothing |

Every call still carries its own `costPaise` in the job body. It reads `0`, and it is not a
placeholder for a figure that arrives later.

This is why `POST /v1/relay/voice` can accept up to 500 recipients without the cost changing:
what you are buying is the sentence, not the dialing.

## Activation: Rs 200, once, per project

Switching **Relay Voice** on for a project charges 20000 paise the first time it happens for
that project. It buys the project its place on the outbound line and its registration with
the carriers, both of which we are billed for monthly whether or not you place a single call.

<Warning>
  It is not a phone number. A number of your own is
  [rented separately](/relay-voice/numbers), monthly, and is optional.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Switching it off and on again is free" icon="toggle-on">
    The fee is stamped on the project the first time it is paid and the stamp is never
    cleared. Flipping the toggle later, however many times, costs nothing.
    `relayActivationPaid` on the project tells you which projects have already paid.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A second project is a second Rs 200" icon="folder-plus">
    Each project is registered with the carriers on its own, so one project's standing is
    never another project's problem. If you do not need that separation, do not create a
    separate project: one project can serve several campaigns, and recordings are shared
    account-wide anyway.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It is charged before the switch flips" icon="wallet">
    If the wallet cannot cover the fee, the answer is `402 insufficient_balance` with
    `requiredPaise: 20000` and the project is left exactly as it was, rather than switched on
    for free. Top up and flip it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where it appears" icon="receipt">
    In the wallet ledger as a `Debit`, referenced `relay-activation-` followed by the
    project id **with its hyphens removed**: project `8f14e45f-ceea-467a-9a1f-6b3c1a2d4e57`
    gives `relay-activation-8f14e45fceea467a9a1f6b3c1a2d4e57`. Strip the hyphens before
    matching, or the lookup silently finds nothing. It is deliberately **not** in
    `GET /api/v1/usage`: that endpoint reports product consumption, and the activation fee is
    account spend rather than something you consumed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How the money moves

<Steps>
  <Step title="A hold goes on before anything is dialed">
    `newCharacters x 3`, reserved against the wallet the moment the request is accepted.

    If the balance cannot cover it, the send is refused with `402 insufficient_balance` and
    `requiredPaise`. That check happens before any provider is called, so a refused send
    costs nothing at all.
  </Step>

  <Step title="A send that costs nothing skips the wallet entirely">
    Replaying a recording has nothing to hold. Rather than reserving zero, such a request
    touches the wallet not at all: no hold, no debit, and no ledger row. It runs on an empty
    balance, because it genuinely costs nothing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="It settles at what the recording cost">
    Exactly the recording charge, which is what was held, so nothing comes back and nothing
    is owed on top. The outcome of the calls does not enter into it: they were free when the
    job was accepted and they are free when it finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Worked examples

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="First message on a new project">
    A new project, three test numbers, a 65-character announcement. Two people answer.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Activation, once for this project | | 20,000 |
    | Recording, once for this wording | 65 x 3 | 195 |
    | Three calls | 3 x 0 | 0 |
    | **Wallet spent** | | **20,195** |

    **Rs 201.95**, of which Rs 200 never happens again for this project. The job's own
    `costPaise` is 195: the activation fee is a separate wallet debit, not part of the job.
    Whether two answered or none did makes no difference to either figure.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Broadcast to 500">
    One 65-character message, 500 numbers, 340 answer. The project is already activated.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Held at send | 65 x 3 | 195 |
    | Recording | 65 x 3 | 195 |
    | 500 calls | 500 x 0 | 0 |
    | **Charged** | | **195** |

    **Rs 1.95** to reach 340 people. The same Rs 1.95 would have reached all 500, or none.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="The same broadcast again">
    Next month, same wording, sent by `audioId` (or by sending the identical text again) to
    the same 500 numbers.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Held at send | nothing to hold | 0 |
    | Recording | already yours | 0 |
    | 500 calls | 500 x 0 | 0 |
    | **Charged** | | **0** |

    **Free.** No hold, no debit, and nothing on the ledger to reconcile: the wallet is not
    touched at all. This is the case worth designing for, and the reason the `audioId` on
    every response is worth keeping.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="One personalized reminder">
    A single 71-character message naming one customer.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Recording, unique to this person | 71 x 3 | 213 |
    | One call | 1 x 0 | 0 |
    | **Charged** | | **213** |

    **Rs 2.13**, answered or not. Personalized sending is where a Relay Voice bill comes
    from: every message is different, so every message is a new recording. Five hundred of
    these cost Rs 1,065; the same words broadcast to the same five hundred cost Rs 2.13.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What costs nothing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Every call, always" icon="phone">
    Answered or not, one or five hundred. Dialing is not metered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Any request that is refused" icon="ban">
    A validation failure (`400`), a product that is switched off (`403`), a wallet that
    cannot cover the hold (`402`), or a speech provider that fails or refuses your text
    (`502`). The balance check runs before any provider is called, and every `502` releases
    the hold in full. A refused send never costs a paisa.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Replaying a recording" icon="rotate-left">
    Preparing the audio is free on every send after the first, whether you replay by
    `audioId` or send the identical text and voice again. Since the calls are free too, such
    a send costs nothing whatsoever.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Duplicate numbers in one request" icon="copy">
    The same person written twice in one `to` array is normalized, deduplicated and dialed
    once. `recipients` on the response tells you how many the request really had.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reading jobs, listing voices, playing samples, test calls" icon="magnifying-glass">
    `GET /v1/relay/voice/{jobId}` and `GET /v1/relay/voice/voices` are free and unmetered,
    poll them as often as you like. The dashboard's in-browser voice samples are free, and so
    is its **test call**: it rings a real phone, replaying a recording you already have, so
    there is neither a call charge nor a synthesis charge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A job we could not finish" icon="rotate">
    If the service restarts mid-job, the job ends as `Failed` with `error: "interrupted"` and
    nothing is redialed. You are charged for the recording, if this job made it, and nothing
    else.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Where to see what you spent

| Surface | Shows |
|---|---|
| The job body, `costPaise` | The final charge for that job: the recording charge if this job made it, and `0` if it replayed one. |
| `GET /api/v1/wallet/transactions` | The ledger. A Relay Voice job that made a recording appears as a `Hold` then a `Debit`, referenced by its `rvj_…` id. A job that replayed one appears not at all. The activation fee is a `Debit` referenced `relay-activation-<projectId>`; a number rental is referenced `relay-number-<numberId>`. |
| `GET /api/v1/usage?product=relay_voice` | Per day, project and voice. `units` is calls placed, which is a volume rather than a bill: `costPaise` is the money, and it moves with characters, not calls. |

<Tip>
  Estimating a campaign before you run it: `characters x 3`, once per **distinct** message,
  plus `20000` if the project has never had Relay Voice switched on, plus `23600` a month for
  each number you rent. The number of people you call does not enter the arithmetic.
</Tip>
