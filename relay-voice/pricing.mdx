---
title: "Relay Voice pricing"
description: "Three charges and nothing else: Rs 200 once per project, 40 paise per answered call, 3 paise per character of newly recorded speech."
---

Relay Voice has exactly three charges. Every amount below is in integer paise, drawn from the
one prepaid wallet your account shares across both products.

| Charge | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | **20000 paise (Rs 200)** | Once, the first time Relay Voice is switched on for a project. |
| Answered call | **40 paise** | Per call that was actually picked up. |
| New speech | **3 paise per character** | Once, when a recording is prepared. Never again for that recording. |

There is no monthly fee, no per-recipient fee for numbers that did not answer, no charge for
reading a job, and no charge for listing voices.

## Activation: Rs 200, once, per project

Switching **Relay Voice** on for a project charges 20000 paise the first time it happens for
that project.

What it buys is a rented caller-id number of that project's own: the number your recipients
see when the phone rings, and the number the carriers associate with your traffic. We are
billed for that number monthly whether or not you place a single call, which is what the fee
covers.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Switching it off and on again is free" icon="toggle-on">
    The fee is stamped on the project the first time it is paid and the stamp is never
    cleared. The number stays rented, so flipping the toggle later, however many times, costs
    nothing. `relayActivationPaid` on the project tells you which projects have already paid.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A second project is a second Rs 200" icon="folder-plus">
    Because it is a second number. Projects get separate caller ids on purpose: one project's
    standing with the carriers should not be another project's problem, and a recipient
    should be able to tell your apps apart by the number that called them.

    If you do not need a separate number, do not create a separate project. One project can
    serve several campaigns, and recordings are shared account-wide anyway.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It is charged before the switch flips" icon="wallet">
    If the wallet cannot cover the fee, the answer is `402 insufficient_balance` with
    `requiredPaise: 20000` and the project is left exactly as it was, rather than switched on
    for free. Top up and flip it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where it appears" icon="receipt">
    In the wallet ledger as a `Debit`, referenced `relay-activation-` followed by the
    project id **with its hyphens removed**: project `8f14e45f-ceea-467a-9a1f-6b3c1a2d4e57`
    gives `relay-activation-8f14e45fceea467a9a1f6b3c1a2d4e57`. Strip the hyphens before
    matching, or the lookup silently finds nothing. It is
    deliberately **not** in `GET /api/v1/usage`: that endpoint reports product consumption,
    calls and characters, and the activation fee is account spend rather than something you
    consumed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 40 paise per answered call

One flat price per call that was answered. It does not vary by voice, by circle, by network,
or by how long the call lasted.

| Call status | Meaning | Charged |
|---|---|---|
| `Completed` | Answered. | **40 paise** |
| `Busy` | Engaged. | Nothing |
| `NoAnswer` | Rang out. | Nothing |
| `Failed` | The network or the provider refused it. | Nothing |
| `Cancelled` | Never placed. | Nothing |

Every call carries its own `costPaise` in the job body, so a job read shows you exactly which
recipients you paid for.

## 3 paise per character of new speech

Speech is recorded, not streamed, and a recording is paid for **once**: at 3 paise per
character of the text, counted after leading and trailing whitespace is trimmed.

The 65-character message

> Clinic closed tomorrow for Diwali. We reopen on Thursday at 9 am.

costs `65 x 3 = 195 paise` to record, whether it then goes to one phone or five hundred.

<Note>
  The two halves are priced separately because the two suppliers bill on different axes. The
  call provider charges per call; the speech provider charges per character. A single flat
  per-call price would quietly lose money on any message sent to a handful of people, and
  would overcharge every broadcast to subsidize it. Splitting them keeps a broadcast cheap
  and makes personalized sending pay for what it actually consumes.
</Note>

**You do not pay it twice for the same words.** Sending an `audioId`, or re-sending wording
your account has already recorded in the same voice, skips synthesis entirely and the charge
with it. This is the single largest variable in a Relay Voice bill: see
[reusing a recording](/relay-voice/reusing-audio).

<Warning>
  A recording is charged when it is **prepared**, not when it is heard. If you record a new
  message and nobody picks up the phone, the calls cost nothing and the recording still
  costs 3 paise per character. Unanswered calls are free. Unheard recordings are not.
</Warning>

## How the money moves

<Steps>
  <Step title="A hold goes on before anything is dialed">
    `recipients x 40 + newCharacters x 3`, reserved against the wallet the moment the request
    is accepted. That is the worst case: everybody picks up.

    If the balance cannot cover it, the send is refused with `402 insufficient_balance` and
    `requiredPaise`. That check happens before any provider is called, so a refused send
    costs nothing at all.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The job runs">
    `estimatedCostPaise` on the response is what is held. `costPaise` stays 0 until the job
    finishes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="It settles against who answered">
    `answeredCalls x 40 + recordingCharge`. The difference between that and the hold goes
    straight back to the balance.

    The settled figure is always at or below the hold, because the hold assumed everyone
    would answer and priced the recording up front. A job that ends up costing nothing at all
    is released rather than debited, so no ledger row claims a movement of zero.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Worked examples

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="First message on a new project">
    A new project, three test numbers, a 65-character announcement. Two people answer.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Activation, once for this project | | 20,000 |
    | Recording, once for this wording | 65 x 3 | 195 |
    | Answered calls | 2 x 40 | 80 |
    | One phone rang out | 1 x 0 | 0 |
    | **Wallet spent** | | **20,275** |

    **Rs 202.75**, of which Rs 200 never happens again for this project. The job's own
    `costPaise` is 275: the activation fee is a separate wallet debit, not part of the job.

    The hold at send time was 315 paise (3 x 40 + 195); 40 paise came back.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Broadcast to 500">
    One 65-character message, 500 numbers, 340 answer. The project is already activated.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Held at send | 500 x 40 + 195 | 20,195 |
    | Recording | 65 x 3 | 195 |
    | Answered calls | 340 x 40 | 13,600 |
    | Released back to the wallet | 160 unanswered x 40 | -6,400 |
    | **Charged** | | **13,795** |

    **Rs 137.95** for 340 people reached. The 160 phones that did not pick up cost nothing.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="The same broadcast again">
    Next month, same wording, sent by `audioId` (or by sending the identical text again) to
    the same 500 numbers. This time 372 answer.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Held at send | 500 x 40 + 0 | 20,000 |
    | Recording | already yours | 0 |
    | Answered calls | 372 x 40 | 14,880 |
    | **Charged** | | **14,880** |

    **Rs 148.80.** This is **more** than last month's Rs 137.95, because 32 more people
    picked up and every answered call costs the same 40 paise. What reuse saved is the
    recording: 195 paise that a fresh synthesis would have added on top. Reuse never makes a
    send cheaper than the calls it places, it removes the one charge that is not a call.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Nobody answers">
    A new 65-character recording sent to 100 numbers at a bad hour. Not one person picks up.

    | Line | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | Held at send | 100 x 40 + 195 | 4,195 |
    | Answered calls | 0 x 40 | 0 |
    | Recording | 65 x 3 | 195 |
    | Released back to the wallet | | -4,000 |
    | **Charged** | | **195** |

    **Rs 1.95** for a hundred calls nobody took. Had it been a replay of an existing
    recording, the charge would have been **zero** and the whole hold would have gone back.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="One personalized reminder">
    A single 71-character message naming one customer.

    | Outcome | Working | Paise |
    |---|---|---:|
    | They answer | 71 x 3 + 40 | 253 |
    | They do not | 71 x 3 + 0 | 213 |

    **Rs 2.53** or **Rs 2.13**. Note how little of that is the call: on one-off personalized
    messages you are mostly buying speech, which is why sending 500 of them costs six times
    what the same 500 calls cost as a broadcast.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What costs nothing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Any request that is refused" icon="ban">
    A validation failure (`400`), a product that is switched off (`403`), a wallet that
    cannot cover the hold (`402`), or a speech provider that fails or refuses your text
    (`502`). The balance check runs before any provider is called, and every `502` releases
    the hold in full. A refused send never costs a paisa.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Phones that do not pick up" icon="phone-slash">
    `Busy`, `NoAnswer`, `Failed` and `Cancelled` calls are all free. You are billed on
    answers, not on attempts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Duplicate numbers in one request" icon="copy">
    The same person written twice in one `to` array is normalized, deduplicated, dialed once
    and charged once. `recipients` on the response tells you how many the request really had.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Replaying a recording" icon="rotate-left">
    Preparing the audio is free on every send after the first, whether you replay by
    `audioId` or send the identical text and voice again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reading jobs, listing voices, playing samples" icon="magnifying-glass">
    `GET /v1/relay/voice/{jobId}` and `GET /v1/relay/voice/voices` are free and unmetered,
    poll them as often as you like. The dashboard's in-browser voice samples are free too.

    The dashboard's **test call** is not: it rings a real phone, so it costs the usual 40
    paise for the call. It carries no synthesis charge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A job we could not finish" icon="rotate">
    If the service restarts mid-job, the job ends as `Failed` with `error: "interrupted"`,
    nothing is redialed, and the undelivered calls are refunded. You are charged for the
    calls that were genuinely answered, and for the recording, and nothing else.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Where to see what you spent

| Surface | Shows |
|---|---|
| The job body, `costPaise` | The final charge for that job: answered calls plus the recording charge if this job made it. Already includes the recording, so never add it on top. |
| `GET /api/v1/wallet/transactions` | The ledger. A Relay Voice job appears as a `Hold`, then a `Debit` and a `HoldRelease`, all referenced by its `rvj_…` id. The activation fee is a `Debit` referenced `relay-activation-<projectId>`. |
| `GET /api/v1/usage?product=relay_voice` | Per day, project and voice. `units` is calls placed and `costPaise` is money settled. `units - succeededUnits - failedUnits` is the number still in flight: asked for, holding money, not yet finished. |

<Tip>
  Estimating a campaign before you run it: `characters x 3` once per distinct message, plus
  `40 x` the number of people you expect to answer, plus `20000` if the project has never had
  Relay Voice switched on. Everything else is zero.
</Tip>
