**40 paise per message to an Indian number.** Every other country has a price of its own,
listed on [SMS prices by country](/relay-sms/countries).

Charged from the same wallet as Manifold AI and the other CPaaS channels, with no subscription, no minimum and no
per-project fee.

## India

**40 paise per message sent.** One flat rate, whether the message goes out under your own
header or under Tristack's.

## Outside India

A price per message for each country, charged once the message is accepted for delivery.
Sending abroad needs the project's **International SMS** switch on; see
[Sending to other countries](/relay-sms/index#sending-to-other-countries).

How a country's price is set: each country is priced in US dollars, because that is how
SMS abroad is charged, and converted to rupees at the day's exchange rate, rounded up to the
next 50 paise. No country costs less than Rs 2.00 a message.

**It moves with the rupee.** The exchange rate is read daily, and it is the same rate Manifold
AI bills at, so a price abroad can change by a little from one day to the next. The rate is
fixed for each request, and `costPaise` on the response is what that message cost.

The live list at today's rate is in the dashboard, on the project's **SMS** tab under
**CPaaS**, and at [`GET /v1/relay/sms/countries`](/api-reference/relay-sms/countries). Read
the endpoint rather than hardcoding a price.

## What is not charged

- **Verifying.** Unlimited within the attempt cap, and free.
- **A resend inside the window.** Asking again for the same number within a minute returns the
  existing code and sends nothing, so nothing is charged. The response says `"reused": true`.
- **A refused send.** If the message is not accepted for delivery, the hold is released whole
  and you are not charged. `costPaise` in the response is what you actually paid.
- **A refusal before sending.** An invalid number, a rate limit, a template that cannot be
  filled, a number abroad with International SMS off, or a country Tristack cannot reach yet
  all cost nothing, because nothing was sent.

## What is charged even when it does not arrive

A message that is accepted and then dropped by the operator **is** charged. Delivery is
reported honestly rather than quietly refunded: `GET /v1/relay/sms/otp/{id}` shows
`delivery.status` as `failed` or `blocked`, and the usage view reports the cost of those
messages separately so it is visible rather than buried.

The commonest cause on your own header is a template mismatch, which is why Tristack checks the
rendered message against your registered text before spending anything. See
[Using your own header](/relay-sms/dlt-setup).

Abroad there are no delivery reports yet, so `delivery.status` reads `unknown` for every
message there. A message abroad is charged once it is accepted, as in India.

## Unlike WhatsApp, there is no second bill

The WhatsApp channel charges 20 paise and Meta bills you separately for WhatsApp messages. SMS
does not work that way: 40 paise in India, or the country's price abroad, is the whole cost of
the message, and no operator or aggregator will invoice you for anything.

If you bring your own DLT registration, your operator may charge you for the registration
itself. That is between you and them and does not reach your Tristack wallet.

## Seeing what you spent

Per-message lines appear in **Usage**, with the sender header as the item, so traffic on your
own header and traffic on Tristack's are told apart. A message abroad has no header, so its
item names the country instead, as in `Abroad (DE)`. The Indian rate is also published at
`GET /api/v1/usage/rates`, and the prices abroad at `GET /v1/relay/sms/countries`, so nothing
has to hardcode them.
