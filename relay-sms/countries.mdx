What one code by SMS costs outside India, country by country. India is not in this list: an
Indian number is **40 paise**, always. See [SMS pricing](/relay-sms/pricing).

These prices were computed at **Rs 94.48 per US dollar on 6 November 2026**. A price abroad
moves with the rupee, so what you are charged today may differ slightly from this page. The
live list, at today's rate, is in the dashboard (the project's **SMS** tab, under **CPaaS**)
and at [`GET /v1/relay/sms/countries`](/api-reference/relay-sms/countries). What a message
actually cost is the `costPaise` on its own response.

Every country here needs the project's **International SMS** switch. See
[Sending to other countries](/relay-sms/index#sending-to-other-countries) for the switch, and
for when numbers abroad are answered.

## Countries not available yet

Some countries accept SMS only from a sender registered in that country, and others only from
a registered local number (the United States and Canada among them). Tristack has not made
those registrations yet, so a number in one of these 61 countries is refused with
`400 sms_country_unavailable` before anything is charged. In the live list they read
`available: false` and `needsRegistration: true`.

Argentina, Australia, Bahamas, Belarus, Belgium, Brazil, Canada, Cayman Islands, Chile, China, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Egypt, El Salvador, Guam, Guatemala, Hungary, Indonesia, Ireland, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kenya, Kuwait, Liberia, Malaysia, Marshall Islands, Micronesia, Monaco, Mozambique, New Zealand, Nicaragua, Oman, Paraguay, Peru, Philippines, Puerto Rico, Qatar, Republic of the Congo, Russia, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, South Africa, South Korea, Spain, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, Turkey, Turkmenistan, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, United States, Uruguay, US Virgin Islands, Venezuela, Vietnam, Zambia.
